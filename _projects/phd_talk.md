Plasma potential is a fundamental, spontaneous and hardly controllable property of plasmas. It induces an electric field E, which impacts the dynamics of magnetized plasmas through the <i>E x B</i> drift. Its control is thus of prime importance for reliable operation of new plasma technologies. 

We investigated the influence of current injection in a magnetized plasma column on plasma potential. The large dataset was leveraged to conduct an in-depth comparison with a theoretical model based on voltage drop computation using Ohm's law in this anisotropic medium. It resulted in a good understanding of the radial voltage drop, paving the way for predictable plasma potential control using a hot cathode.

This poster is accompanied by a publication in <i>Plasma Sources Science and Technology</i> {% cite PSST_2025 %}. It was presented at <u>MagNetUS 2022</u> (online), and <u>EPS Plasma 2022</u> (online), where I was awarded <b>Best poster presentation</b>.
