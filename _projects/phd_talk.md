Radial transport, azimuthal waves and instabilities cause major challenges plasma propulsion, plasma wakefield acceleration or fusion devices. Control of magnetized plasma properties is desirable yet complex. 

In this PhD defence, I present the use of an emissive cathode as a new control tool, and the fundamental understanding of the helicon plasma operational stability. Experimental perspective on cathode design, plasma potential control model refinement and the implementation of a novel dual helicon configuration are also presented.

This talk is accompanied by my PhD manuscript {% cite Pagaud_phd %}. It was presented at ENS de Lyon on July 1st, 2024. The jury was composed of 6 members: Cécile Arnas (rapporteur), Stéphane Heuraux (rapporteur), Ivo Furno (examiner, chair), Renaud Gueroult (examiner), Sedina Tsikata (examiner) and Nicolas Plihon (PhD supervisor).
